About the Arcane Gallery

This website is a character gallery based on Arcane. It was created to show different characters from Piltover and Zaun and give a quick look at their roles, personalities, and stories.

Arcane has a lot of interesting characters with different backgrounds and relationships. Some characters come from Piltover, while others come from Zaun, and those differences play a big part in the story. This gallery gives visitors a simple way to learn more about some of the main characters.

What You Can Find Here

On the home page, you can view five different characters and read a short description about each one. The details page gives more information about Jinx, including her background and personality. You can also use the Add Character page to fill out a form for a new character.
