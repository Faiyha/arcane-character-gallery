Jinx

Jinx is a character from Zaun who is known for being creative, unpredictable, and skilled at making weapons. She has been through a lot since she was young, which affects the way she thinks and acts. One of the biggest parts of her story is her complicated relationship with her sister Vi.

Quick Info

Universe: Arcane

Region: Zaun

Real Name: Powder

Role: Inventor and fighter

Sister: Vi

Backstory

Jinx grew up in Zaun with her sister Vi. When she was younger, she was known as Powder and often struggled with feeling like she was not as strong or useful as the people around her. The events she goes through while growing up have a major effect on her and play a big role in the person she becomes later.

Personality

Jinx is very creative, emotional, and unpredictable. She is talented at building weapons and gadgets, but she can also act impulsively when she feels angry, afraid, or betrayed. Even though she causes a lot of chaos, many of her actions are connected to her past and the people she cares about.
